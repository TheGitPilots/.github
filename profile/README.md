## 🚀 About Us

We are **Axiom Vessel** — a team of developers and students at **Daffodil International University (DIU)**, building practical, open-source tools to improve the academic experience for our peers and beyond.

We believe in learning by doing, and every project we ship is a step toward that goal.

---

## 🏗️ What We're Building

### 📚 [DIU Notes Buddy](https://diunotesbuddy.live/)

> *Helping Daffodil International University students excel in their studies. A note sharing site at your service.*

**DIU Notes Buddy** is our flagship project — a free, community-driven note-sharing platform built specifically for **Software Engineering** students at DIU.

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Notes Repository** | Browse and access course notes organized by subject |
| 📁 **Other Resources** | Supplementary study materials and references |
| 🔍 **Easy Navigation** | Clean, intuitive web interface for quick access |
| 🌍 **Live & Free** | Publicly accessible at [diunotesbuddy.live](https://diunotesbuddy.live/) |
